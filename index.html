Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Spryzen's zero-trust offline scanner.

🔒 Are my server logs uploaded to your servers or any third-party?

Absolutely NOT. The analysis engine runs 100% locally inside your browser’s volatile RAM using client-side JavaScript. You can turn off your Wi-Fi or put your computer in Airplane Mode, and the scanner will continue to function flawlessly.

⚡ Can this handle massive multi-gigabyte production logs (10GB+)?

Yes. Spryzen uses a stream-chunked sliding window architecture that processes large files in small 8MB buffers, scanning over 100,000 requests per second without exceeding 50MB of browser memory.

🎯 How does Spryzen eliminate false positives?

Unlike primitive scanners that flag isolated words like "union" or "select", Spryzen uses strict AST grammar boundary checks to verify genuine execution syntax violations before flagging any request.
